About This Curriculum

A clinical-reasoning library built from Adams & Victor's Principles of Neurology (12e) and Merritt's Neurology (14e). Every topic is offered in two tracks, each with a printable Reference and an Interactive lesson:

General Physician — recognition, red flags, when to refer Neurology Residency — mechanism, trials, algorithms, board depth

The residency track is rolling out module by module. Where a residency lesson is still In development, the General track covers the topic in the meantime.
