🧠 Neurology Curriculum

Self-Study Modules for General Physicians

About This Curriculum

A comprehensive clinical-reasoning-focused self-study series built from two authoritative texts:

  • Adams & Victor's Principles of Neurology (12th Edition)
  • Merritt's Neurology (14th Edition)

27 modules covering core neurology topics for general physicians seeking refresher material, recognition of key presentations, red flags, and when-to-refer guidance. Not intended as neurology residency preparation.
